Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email me and I'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

packthevan.dev@gmail.com

The quickest way to report a bug is in the game: Settings → Report a problem. It adds your game version and iPhone model for you, so I can fix it faster.

Common questions

I bought "Remove ads" but I'm seeing ads again

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Videos you choose to watch for hints or coins are still available after Remove ads, but they only play when you tap them.

I bought coins but didn't get them

Email me with the date of the purchase. Forwarding Apple's receipt email helps, but leave out any payment details. Coin packs are used up once they're added, so Restore purchases can't bring back coins you've already spent.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress is saved on your iPhone, and updates never reset it. Deleting the app deletes your progress. If you move to a new iPhone by restoring from an iCloud or computer backup, your progress normally comes with it.

How do I earn 3 stars?

Finish a level without using any hints. Using a hint still completes the level, with 2 stars. You can replay any level later to earn the third star.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In Europe, the UK and some US states, you can also change your choice in the game under Settings → Privacy choices. See the privacy policy for details.

The music or sound isn't playing

Check that your iPhone's silent switch is off and the volume is up, then check Settings in the game: Music and Sound effects each have their own switch and volume slider.
