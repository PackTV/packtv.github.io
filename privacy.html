Privacy Policy

Pack the Van for iPhone · Effective 23 October 2026

Pack the Van is made by an independent developer in Australia ("I", "me"), who is also its seller on the App Store. This policy explains what information the game uses, who receives it, and the choices you have.

In short: there are no accounts, and I don't collect your name, email or location. Your progress stays on your iPhone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device and usage information to show and measure those ads. You can limit this, and you can buy "Remove ads".

Information stored on your iPhone

Your progress, stars, coins, items you've unlocked and your settings are saved on your iPhone so you can carry on where you left off. This information is not sent to me. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, it may be included in that backup, under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • device and app information, such as the iPhone model, iOS version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic information, such as crashes or ads that fail to load.

If you allow tracking, and where the law requires it you consent, Google may use this information to show ads based on your interests. Otherwise Google shows non-personalised ads. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may process information in the United States and other countries.

I don't receive your advertising identifier or any information that identifies you personally. AdMob only gives me totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking: the first time you play, iOS asks whether to allow tracking. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland: you'll be asked for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • United States: where your state's privacy law gives you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted ads, Google's privacy message in the game lets you do so, and you can change it under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Remove ads: buying "Remove ads" in the shop removes the banner and between-level ads. Videos you choose to watch for hints or bonus coins stay available, and only play when you tap them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. I never see your payment details or Apple Account. Apple only tells the game which items were bought, so it can give you your coins or remove ads. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Problem reports and emails

If you use Settings → Report a problem, the game prepares an email in your own email app. It includes what you wrote, the game version, your iPhone model and iOS version, screen size, the time, whether ads are working, and, if you tick the box, the layout of the level you were playing. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

If you email me, I receive your email address and anything you include. I use it only to help with your question or fix the problem, and I delete it when it's no longer needed. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Children

Pack the Van is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has sent me personal information, email me and I'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it's used. Because I don't hold information about players other than emails you send me, contact me for anything about those emails. For information collected by Google for ads, use the choices above or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
